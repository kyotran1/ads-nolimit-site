"AI agent" sounds grand, but for a shop owner the idea is simple: it is an **AI assistant that looks after your messages**. It reads your shop’s documents to answer customers, and it can take care of a few small tasks, such as saving customer details to a spreadsheet or letting your staff know.

## How is an AI agent different from an old chatbot?

Old-style chatbots follow a decision tree: the customer taps "See prices" and the bot sends a canned reply. Type anything outside the script and the bot is stuck.

An AI agent is different in three ways:

- **It understands questions written naturally.** A message like "got size M in beige?" still makes sense to it.
- **It answers from your shop’s own documents:** price list, returns policy, delivery fees, opening hours.
- **It gets things done, not just answers:** it saves phone numbers to Google Sheets, alerts staff on Zalo or Telegram, checks stock and drafts orders.

## What do shop owners use AI agents for?

1. **Answering repeat questions.** How much is it, is this size in stock, how long does delivery take, where is the shop. These are usually most of the day’s messages.
2. **Keeping customers after hours.** Someone who messages at 11 pm gets an answer straight away and is asked for a phone number, instead of waiting until morning.
3. **Keeping every lead in one place.** Customer details from your Facebook page, Zalo OA and website go into the same sheet instead of getting lost in the inbox.
4. **Alerting the right person at the right time.** A new lead, a new draft order, a customer who needs in-depth advice: staff get a notification on their phone straight away.
5. **Handing over to a person when needed.** Difficult questions, complaints, or a customer who wants a human: the AI passes the conversation on instead of trying to answer.

## What not to hand over to AI yet

AI agents are good at repetitive work backed by clear documents. They should not yet handle things like:

- Sensitive expert advice: health, skin conditions, legal matters.
- Deciding on discounts or offers outside your policy.
- Customers who are upset and need empathy and a decision from someone with authority.

A good AI agent knows when to stop and call a person.

## What to prepare before you start

- **Your current price list**, fully up to date.
- **The questions customers ask most**, with your shop’s standard answers. Scroll back through a week of your Facebook inbox and you will have them.
- **Your policies:** returns, warranty, delivery, opening hours.
- **Who gets notified:** who receives new leads, who handles orders, who replies when the AI hands over.

## Start small

You don’t need to do everything at once. The safest way is to start with **one channel** (Messenger, Zalo OA or website chat) and **one set of FAQs**, run it for a few weeks, see where the AI’s answers fall short and fill the gaps. Once that is working, add more channels and tasks such as stock checks or draft orders.
