In 2026, two new laws directly affect how shop owners use AI and keep customer information. This article only summarises the key points, with sources listed at the end.

## Law on Artificial Intelligence (No. 134/2025/QH15)

- **In force from 1 March 2026.**
- The law **requires AI-generated content to be labelled**.

For shop owners, this covers very familiar content: product photos composited with AI, ad videos with an AI voice-over, illustrations for Facebook posts.

## Personal Data Protection Law (No. 91/2025/QH15)

- **In force from 1 January 2026.**
- The law **gives people the right to request deletion of their personal data**.

For shop owners, "personal data" is close at hand: customer names, phone numbers and delivery addresses in your Facebook inbox, your Excel order file, your spa’s customer book.

## Practical tips for shop owners

These are steps worth taking to stay ahead, not a complete list of legal obligations:

1. **Add a note when you use AI-generated images or video.** A short line such as "Image created with the help of AI" on the post or on your website.
2. **Know where your customer data lives.** Facebook page, Zalo, Excel files, sales software, staff phones. If you don’t know where it is, you can’t delete it when a customer asks.
3. **Only ask for what you really need.** Delivery needs a name, phone number and address. If you don’t need something, don’t ask for it.
4. **Have a privacy policy page** that says what your shop collects, why, and who customers can contact to ask for deletion.
5. **If you use an AI agent to answer customers**, let them know they are talking to an AI assistant, and store their details in accounts your shop manages itself.
